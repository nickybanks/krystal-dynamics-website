About Krystal Dynamics

Hi, I'm Nick - the developer behind Krystal Dynamics.

Krystal Dynamics is an independent audio software project focused on building clean, precise and professional tools for modern mixing and mastering.

The project started during my graduation in Sound and Music Computing at the University of Milan (UniMI), Italy. As a solo developer, these plugins represent several months of work exploring digital signal processing and have evolved into a suite of practical tools for producers and engineers.

Every plugin is designed, developed, tested and maintained entirely by me. I put strong emphasis on sound quality, CPU efficiency and workflow simplicity, avoiding unnecessary complexity while keeping full control over the dynamics.

I believe that professional tools should be accessible to everyone, which is why all plugins are completely free. However, developing and maintaining audio software requires a significant amount of time, research and continuous improvement.

User feedback and community support play a fundamental role in allowing this project to grow and continue evolving.
